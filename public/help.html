Browse listings, open one you like, pick your check-in and check-out dates, select the number of guests, then click "Book Now". You must be logged in to complete a booking.

Go to My Trips from the menu after logging in. All your upcoming and past bookings are listed there with dates, location, and status.

Yes — you can book on behalf of someone else. Just enter the number of guests correctly and coordinate directly with the host for any special arrangements.

After booking you'll see a confirmation page with all your stay details. You can find these anytime in My Trips. The host is notified automatically.

Go to My Trips, find the booking you want to cancel, and click "Cancel Trip". Note that cancellation removes the booking immediately — this action is irreversible.

Cancellation policies vary per listing. Generally, cancellations made 48+ hours before check-in qualify for a full refund. Check the listing page for its specific policy before booking.

Refunds typically process within 5–7 business days depending on your bank or payment method. You'll receive a confirmation once the refund has been initiated.

No — only upcoming trips (where check-out has not passed) can be cancelled. Past bookings are automatically marked "Completed" and cannot be modified.
